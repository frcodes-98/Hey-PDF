Private document tools

DocForge is a static, browser-only toolkit. There is no backend, no account system, and no file upload endpoint.

What stays on your device

When you open a PDF or image, the browser reads it into memory, runs the selected tool with client-side libraries, and offers a download. Your files are not sent to DocForge servers — because there are none.

Honest capabilities

Merge, split, rotate, organize, delete pages, visual edit overlays, sign (draw or stamp), crop a signature from a page, PDF↔images, and images→PDF are strong client-side features. Compress re-encodes pages (best-effort). Extract text, PDF to Word, and PDF to Excel use the PDF text layer, then in-browser OCR (English) for scanned pages. Word and Excel downloads are simple files — not layout-faithful conversions. Edit PDF adds overlays — it does not rewrite original text in place. Redact can use a chosen color, pick a pixel from the page, or detect nearby paper color. Cropped signatures may be stored in localStorage so Sign PDF can reuse them on this device.

Theme preference

The optional theme toggle stores a single preference key in localStorage. Crop signature can also keep a PNG stamp in localStorage for Sign PDF. No analytics or tracking cookies are used by this project.

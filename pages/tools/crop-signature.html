Crop signature

Draw a box around a signature on the page and download it as a PNG you can reuse in Sign PDF.

Drag on the page to draw a crop box.
1 / 1

Options

This copies a high-resolution crop from the page. Background removal is best-effort for dark ink on light paper — it does not detect signatures.

Use stronger if pale paper still shows. Keep paper if the ink is light or the background is busy.

Removes ink that sits clearly outside the printed signature lines, and tries to erase those lines so the PNG is just the signature. Strokes that overlap a line may get a little thinner. Busy or colored forms can be missed — then all ink is kept.

Open a PDF, go to the signed page, then draw a box around the signature to preview the PNG.

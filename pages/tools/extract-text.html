Extract text

Extract text from a PDF, including scanned pages, then download a Word file or plain text. OCR runs locally in your browser.

Options

Text-layer PDFs extract instantly. Scanned pages run OCR in your browser (English). Word export is a simple document from that text — it does not keep PDF layout, and signatures stay as pixels on the page (use Crop signature for a PNG).

OCR stays on this device. Accuracy varies with scan quality.

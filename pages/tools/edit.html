Edit PDF

Add text, tick and cross stamps, images, signatures, highlights, and redactions on a live page preview. Drag to place, then download.

1 / 1

Options

Edits are overlays. Existing PDF text is not rewritten in place. Signatures are drawings or images you place — not legal e-sign, and existing signatures are not detected.

Used for new text overlays. Word-like fonts are embedded in the download. If Microsoft Word is installed, the live preview can use the real fonts.

Select a tool, then click or drag on the page. Tick and Cross place checkbox-style stamps. Redact can use a color picker, pick from the page, or detect the paper color. Sign opens a pad to draw or upload. Drag objects to move; use corners to resize.

Used for the next redaction. Pick samples a pixel on the page. Detect guesses the paper color.

Smaller values are better for captions and form labels.

Black covers the area. Pick samples a pixel; Detect matches paper around the box.
